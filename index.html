FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common travel questions

How long does it take to get a visa?
Processing times vary by country. Canada & UK take 15-30 days, Schengen 10-15 days, and e-visas like Turkey & Malaysia take 3-5 days. We'll give you exact timelines after document submission.
What documents do I need for a visit visa?
Generally: valid passport (6+ months), passport-size photos, bank statements (6 months), travel itinerary, hotel booking, and invitation letter (if applicable). We'll provide a custom checklist based on your destination.
Do you guarantee visa approval?
No agency can guarantee visa approval as it's up to the embassy. However, we have a 90%+ success rate and provide expert document verification to minimize rejection risk. We'll guide you every step of the way.
Can I pay in installment for packages?
Yes! We offer flexible payment plans for most travel packages. Contact our team to discuss a payment schedule that works for you.
Do you offer hotel + flight combo deals?
Absolutely! We specialize in custom packages that include flights, hotels, and even visa support. Just tell us your destination and budget, and we'll create the perfect deal.
