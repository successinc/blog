Work-permit enquiries

Overseas work opportunities need careful checks.

We can discuss general document-preparation questions. Verify the employer, recruitment terms and permit process through official channels before making decisions.

Take time to verify

A real opportunity should be clear on paper.

Work-permit rules depend on country, role and employer. Success Inc. does not promise jobs, placement, permits, earnings or processing timelines. Verify every offer independently.

  • Confirm the employer’s legal identity and role in writing
  • Understand who handles recruitment and official filings
  • Review salary, hours, accommodation, deductions and contract terms
  • Check the permit route with the destination authority
  • Request an itemised list of fees and receipts

Before you pay or share documents

Independent checks protect you.

Do not rely on a poster, verbal promise or social-media message as proof of a job. Ask for the full written offer and confirm the recruitment route with the appropriate official bodies.

  • Never pay a fee you cannot explain
  • Do not hand over original documents without a lawful reason and receipt
  • Keep copies of contracts, messages and payment records

Owner-supplied recruitment posters

Malaysia roles shown in the supplied artwork.

These images are displayed as received to identify the advertised sectors. They are not verified job vacancies, offers or guarantees. One poster mentions RM 1,700; that figure is not confirmed here and may not represent net pay, current terms or any available role.

Please verify independently: ask for the named employer, signed contract, job location, total recruitment fees, salary and deductions in writing. Check the current legal process with the relevant Bangladesh and destination-country authorities before paying or submitting documents.
